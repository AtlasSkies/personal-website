The second Salem stepped foot on the space station, the predictions for the future changed. Salem Mora follows a 14-year-old girl who accidentally bumps into a strange man and follows him to a technologically advanced society hidden at the edge of space. She's surrounded by people who hate Earthlings — and the teleporter she used won't work again for another year. She tries to lay low and wait. Except a weapon owned by a powerful man contacts her. And an even more powerful woman wants it. So she's left with two choices: pretend nothing is happening and wait out the year, or find the weapon and try to save a world that would hate her if they found out where she was from.
