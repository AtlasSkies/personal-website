The second Salem stepped foot on the space station, the predictions for the future changed. Salem Mora follows a 14-year-old girl who accidentally bumps into a strange man and follows him to a technologically advanced society hidden at the edge of space. Surrounded by people who resent Earthlings — and with the teleporter she used broken for another year — she tries to lay low and wait. Except a weapon owned by a powerful man contacts her. And an even more powerful woman wants it. Two choices: pretend nothing is happening, or find the weapon and try to save a world that would hate her if they knew where she was from.
